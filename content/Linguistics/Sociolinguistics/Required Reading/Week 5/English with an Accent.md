### Teaching Children how to Discriminate

There has been controversy over the use of accents to denote a person's bad character. Such stereotyping needs to be taken seriously because children learn how to make judgements about people based on their voice from TV.

> It is first observably true that somehow, children learn not only how to use variation in their own language, but also how to interpret social vari­ation in the language of others. They do this with or without exposure to television and film, but in the current day, few children grow up without this exposure.

#### Talking the talk

Actors bring their accents to a role. Sometimes roles are written for them or at times they are trained to use different accents.

> What is particularly relevant and interesting in this context, however, is the way that actors attempt to manipulate language as a tool in the construction of character, whether or not they are successful. 

Accents are sometimes used as a shorthand to show that an actor is speaking a foreign language.
